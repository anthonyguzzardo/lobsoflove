## 1. Prediction-market mainstreaming: adjacent needs and who serves them

### Takeaway
Real-money prediction markets went mainstream in the past 12 months (Polymarket US app Dec 2025, Kalshi distributed inside Robinhood, Meta building a points-based "Arena" reported June 2026), and the integrity/verification layer has become an acute, publicly-litigated problem. The adjacent non-money layer (social forecasting with friends, reputation-based "receipts", community oracles) is thinly populated by tiny unfunded apps, and Meta's own entry would be points-based — which is simultaneously validation and a looming incumbent.

### Cited Findings
- Kalshi and Polymarket account for about 97.5% of the prediction-market sector; Kalshi overtook Polymarket with $39.5B total notional volume vs $29.2B — [Track360 operator landscape 2026](https://track360.io/blog/prediction-market-operators-landscape-polymarket-kalshi-alternatives-2026); [SailGP prediction markets guide](https://sailgp.com/prediction-markets)
- Kalshi embedded its markets in Robinhood, which now drives over half of Kalshi's volume — [SI.com apps like Robinhood](https://www.si.com/prediction-markets/reviews/apps-like-robinhood)
- Polymarket launched a US app on December 3, 2025 after acquiring CFTC-registered QCEX for $112M in July 2025; initially listed under the App Store sports category, invite-only; waitlist removed May 2026 — [Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/markets/polymarket-rolls-out-us-app); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/crypto-prediction-platform-polymarket-relaunches-162053634.html)
- Polymarket sought CFTC approval on April 28, 2026 to reopen its main exchange to US traders — [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/04/28/polymarket-seeks-cftc-approval-to-reopen-main-exchange-to-u-s-traders)
- Meta "Arena": per a New York Times report (June 24, 2026), Zuckerberg assigned a small internal team to build a standalone prediction-market smartphone app; it "would likely use a points system instead of cash wagers," though Meta "has not ruled out adding real money betting later"; described as experimental, early, and "may never launch"; Meta declined comment. Meta previously ran a points-based "Forecast" app 2020–2022 that was shut down for lack of adoption — [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-meta-arena-prediction-market-app/); [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/tradfi-and-fintech/zuckerberg-orders-meta-to-build-standalone-prediction-market-app-codenamed-arena); [NPR, June 24, 2026](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/24/nx-s1-5869486/meta-prediction-market-app-ai); [FXStreet, dated 2026-06-24](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/meta-ceo-zuckerberg-directs-development-of-standalone-prediction-markets-app-arena-202606240419)
- Integrity crisis: Kalshi on June 10, 2026 rolled out employment-disclosure requirements for high-risk markets, a six-factor risk-scoring framework, whistleblower tools, 24/7 surveillance and quarterly audit-committee reports; it cited "more than 150 investigations this year," "blocked over 100 potential insider trades," "referred more than 20 cases to law enforcement," five disciplinary actions, and three political candidates fined/suspended — [Decrypt, June 10, 2026](https://decrypt.co/370618/kalshi-rolls-out-new-safeguards-after-insider-trading-concerns-hit-prediction-markets)

- First criminal insider-trading case tied to a prediction market was reported April 27, 2026 — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/walterpavlo/2026/04/27/prediction-markets-first-insider-trading-case/)

- Developer surface: Kalshi exposes REST, WebSocket and FIX with Python/TypeScript SDKs; a 30+-project open-source bot ecosystem exists; Kalshi backs a $2M grants program for apps built on the DFlow Prediction Markets API (Solana) — [botforkalshi ecosystem](https://www.botforkalshi.com/blog/open-source-kalshi-bot-ecosystem); [Solana/DFlow](https://solana.com/news/dflow-prediction-markets-api); [predictreport APIs for builders](https://predictreport.io/blog/best-prediction-market-apis-builders)
- Non-money early entrants: PROPS ("Social Prediction Game") by Cronomi LLC — free, no in-app purchases, daily coin allowance, dynamic share pricing, "Props" reputation score, Rookie→Oracle ranks, permanent trade history with shareable "receipts"; initial release February 6 (year not shown on listing; version 1.0.13 dated March 8); "hasn't received enough ratings" to show a score — [PROPS App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/props-social-prediction-game/id6758321633); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cronomi.props&hl=en)

- Other small no-money entrants: SocialStar (private friends/family prediction game), MPP "The social predictor" (sports scores), a Manifund-funded "app for personal predictions with friends" (Brier-score clout) — [SocialStar](https://apps.apple.com/dz/app/socialstar/id6473705189); [MPP](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.monpetitprono.monpetitpronoapp&hl=en_US); [Manifund project](https://manifund.org/projects/app-for-personal-predictions-with-friends?tab=comments)

### Inferences
- "Integrity/verification of real-world outcomes" is now a real, funded need — but it is being served at the exchange level (Kalshi in-house, Polymarket+Chainalysis) and is enterprise/compliance-shaped. A solo mobile dev has no wedge there; the buyers are two exchanges and regulators.
- Oracle services for private communities: no consumer-grade product found. The reason is more "doesn't work" than "no one is here": resolving arbitrary private claims needs either a trusted human arbiter (which is what a group chat already is) or an exchange-grade data feed. The Kalshi/Polymarket APIs do give a free, authoritative resolution source for public events, which a no-money app can piggyback on.
- Sports-adjacent, non-money social forecasting is the cleanest unclaimed slot for this founder: PROPS and SocialStar are tiny and unreviewed, Sleeper Predicts is still "coming soon" after months, and Meta's Arena is points-based but unconfirmed and may never ship (and Meta's Forecast already failed once). The wedge is distribution-shaped, not feature-shaped: a group-chat-native (iMessage/Discord) "receipts" game resolved automatically off Kalshi/Polymarket market settlement, with the AI agent generating and resolving markets from a chat's own conversation. Risk: Meta Arena launching with Instagram distribution; mitigation is going hyper-local (campus/friend-group) where Meta's Forecast failed.
- Creator markets (a creator opening markets for their audience) are structurally blocked for real money (CFTC) and functionally equivalent to Twitch Predictions for points; no funded entrant found outside platforms.

### Gaps
- No public confirmation that Meta Arena has launched or been cancelled as of Oct 2026; all reporting traces to one NYT story (June 24, 2026) plus NPR-cited documents.
- No funding or user numbers found for PROPS, SocialStar or MPP; the Sleeper Predicts launch date was not confirmed.
- Could not find any dedicated "oracle for private communities" startup; absence of evidence, not evidence of absence.

## 2. Proof-of-personhood, anti-AI-content verification, age verification: newly needed consumer/SMB products

### Takeaway
2026 is the year the identity/age layer went from optional to legally mandated for app developers (Texas Jan 1, Utah May 6, Louisiana July 1, Alabama; Apple's Declared Age Range API worldwide Feb 24; Apple's September 2026 "social media capability" declaration; Discord teen-by-default; Australia's under-16 ban Dec 10, 2025). The infrastructure (Apple/Google APIs, k-ID OpenAge, Yoti, Persona) and deepfake detection (enterprise, well funded) are claimed; what is open is the consumer-facing layer — apps designed for the new "verified teen" and "verified human" audiences, and verified-human small communities — which YC is explicitly requesting.

### Cited Findings
- App Store Accountability Acts: Texas effective January 1, 2026; Utah May 6, 2026; Louisiana July 1, 2026; California Digital Age Assurance Act January 1, 2027. App stores must verify age at account creation, get parental consent for minors, and share age category (under 13, 13–15, 16–17, 18+) with developers; developers must assign age ratings per bracket — [Wiley](https://www.wiley.law/alert-State-App-Store-Accountability-Acts-Introduce-New-Obligations-for-App-Developers); [McNees](https://www.mcneeslaw.com/age-verification-laws-app-developers-compliance/); [Baker](https://www.bakerdatacounsel.com/blogs/an-app-developers-guide-to-app-store-age-assurance-laws/)
- Alabama signed HB 161 (App Store Accountability Act) in March 2026 — [Regulatory Oversight](https://www.regulatoryoversight.com/2026/03/alabama-enacts-app-store-accountability-act-requiring-age-verification-and-parental-consent/)
- The Supreme Court allowed Texas to enforce its app-store age-verification law (July 2026) — [SCOTUSblog](https://www.scotusblog.com/2026/07/supreme-court-allows-texas-to-enforce-law-requiring-age-verification-and-parental-consent-on-app/)
- Apple updated its Declared Age Range API (Feb 24, 2026) to return age category (under 13, 13–16, 16–18, 18+) plus age-assurance-method signals and whether regulatory requirements apply; began blocking 18+ app downloads in Australia, Brazil and Singapore for unconfirmed adults; Utah (May 6) and Louisiana (July 1) age categories shared with apps on request; Texas verification began June 2026 — [TechCrunch, Feb 24, 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/24/apple-rolls-out-age-verification-tools-worldwide-to-comply-with-growing-web-of-child-safety-laws/); [Apple Developer news](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=f5zj08ey); [Biometric Update, June 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202606/apple-begins-age-verification-for-texas-app-store-users)

- Reusable age assurance: k-ID's OpenAge initiative (launched Nov 2025) with passkey-based "AgeKey" (from acquired Opale.io); Meta and Socure joined Dec 2025; Persona, Incode and Veratad joined Jan 2026; Yoti has raised over $130M; ISO/IEC 27566-1:2025 is the first global standard; unresolved: who pays providers, friction, government ID vs private — [Biometric Update, Dec 26, 2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202512/one-check-to-rule-them-all-quest-for-reusable-age-assurance-to-ramp-up-in-2026); [Biometric Update, Jan 2026](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202601/persona-incode-veratad-join-k-ids-openage-initiative-for-reusable-age-checks)

### Inferences
- "Verification SDKs" and "deepfake detection" are claimed and capital-intensive — not a solo wedge.
- Genuinely new and under-built: consumer apps that are *native to the age-bracketed world*. Apple now hands every app a reliable age bracket (Declared Age Range) and forces a "social media" declaration; Australia's ban created a documented teen migration to Yope/Lemon8/Coverstar/Locket-style apps that are *not* "social media" under the law. This is "no one is here (yet)" with real demand signals; the risk is the Australian list being "dynamic and agile" and swallowing any successful teen app. A defensible shape is small-group, non-feed, non-algorithmic (private photo messengers, group games) — exactly what survived the ban's definition.
- Verified-human small communities (a "humans-only" product for a local scene, campus, or hobby) is directly requested by YC and signaled by OpenAI's rumored project; a solo dev could use Apple's Face ID/Wallet identity and Declared Age Range plus World ID as optional signals without building biometrics. Skeptical note: no evidence yet that consumers will pay for "verified human" per se; it works better as a property of a product with another reason to exist (events, games, dating-adjacent).
- Content Credentials for ordinary creators remain unclaimed on iOS: Pixel 10 signs by default but iPhone has no reported default C2PA capture, so a "provable-original photo" camera or a "was this made by a human?" viewer for consumers is open — but Apple could close it in one keynote, and consumer willingness to care is unproven ("doesn't work" risk).

- Could not confirm OpenAI's social network status after January 2026 reporting.
- No funding figures found for Yope, Coverstar or Lemon8 growth post-ban beyond App Store rankings.
- Australian ban's "dynamic" list changes after Dec 2025 not found.

## 3. Agentic commerce (ACP, AP2, UCP, Visa/Mastercard, Stripe): what's missing for small merchants

### Takeaway
By October 2026 the merchant-side plumbing is largely solved for anyone on a major platform (Shopify, Stripe ACS with Wix/Squarespace/Etsy/WooCommerce/BigCommerce, PayPal Agent Ready) and the protocol layer (ACP Sept 2025, UCP Jan 2026, AP2) is owned by OpenAI/Stripe/Google/Visa. The unclaimed pieces are in the long tail — self-hosted/local/service merchants, product-data quality, and the consumer side (whose agent, with what trust) — and most are B2B/productivity-shaped, which this founder has excluded.

### Cited Findings
- ACP (OpenAI + Stripe) released September 2025 and powers ChatGPT checkout; UCP (Google + Shopify) released January 2026 and powers Google AI Mode commerce; six protocols define the stack as of April 2026: ACP, UCP, AP2, MCP, A2A, Visa TAP — [Opascope](https://opascope.com/insights/ai-shopping-assistant-guide-2026-agentic-commerce-protocols/); [Traversaal](https://www.traversaal.ai/blog/agentic-commerce-protocols-2026-acp-ucp-mastercard-compared-enterprise-builders)
- AP2 "sits at the PSP layer"; "small merchants will need to do nothing for AP2 in 2026 and probably not in 2027" — [nohacks.co small merchants](https://nohacks.co/blog/agentic-commerce-for-small-merchants)
- Stripe Agentic Commerce Suite announced December 11, 2025: low-code single integration to "sell across AI agents," Shared Payment Tokens; platforms Squarespace, Wix, Etsy, WooCommerce, commercetools, BigCommerce; merchants Coach, Kate Spade, URBN, Revolve, Ashley Furniture, Halara, ABT, Nectar; no pricing given — [Stripe newsroom](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/agentic-commerce-suite); Stripe Sessions 2026 claimed 288 launches — [Stripe Sessions 2026](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/sessions-2026)
- As of September 2026 ACS is live in the US, Canada and parts of Europe — [Clinkbill (secondary)](https://clinkbill.com/blog/agentic-commerce-merchant-stack-psp)
- Shopify "Agentic Storefronts" are on by default for eligible US merchants (covers ACP and UCP); PayPal merchants are auto-enrolled in "Agent Ready"; the main friction is self-hosted stores (WooCommerce needs plugin + developer); merchants must still fix product data (Product + Offer schema, images with alt text) — [nohacks.co](https://nohacks.co/blog/agentic-commerce-for-small-merchants)
- Adobe Q2 2026 report: AI-referred traffic converts 42% better than non-AI on US retailers; Amazon v. Perplexity ruling August 4, 2026 — [nohacks.co](https://nohacks.co/blog/agentic-commerce-for-small-merchants)
- YC Fall 2026 asks for "Crypto infrastructure for the agent economy" (stablecoins, programmable rails for machines); Eze Vidra's Fall 2026 list includes "Identity, payments and reputation for agent economy" — [VC Cafe](https://www.vccafe.com/requests-for-startups-fall-2026-edition/)
- a16z Speedrun Big Ideas: "AI procurement agents across verticals" and one-founder SMB stacks — [LinkedIn summary of a16z 40 ideas](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ivelina-dineva-nft_a16z-just-shared-40-ideas-on-what-they-want-activity-7405233727922094082-X2g3)

### Inferences
- "No one is here" for small merchants is mostly false: the platforms and PSPs closed the gap within 12 months of ACP. What remains is (a) merchants not on a platform — local service businesses, restaurants, pop-ups, farmers-market and campus sellers — who have no catalog for an agent to read, and (b) consumer-side agent trust (which agent, spending limits, receipts). Both are real but B2B/utility-shaped and conflict with the "no productivity tools" constraint.
- The one consumer-fun-shaped wedge: agentic commerce on behalf of a *group* (split orders, group gifts, event supplies) is not in any protocol's launch narrative — but it is unproven demand ("doesn't work" risk: group payments have a long graveyard).
- Skeptical note: AI-referred conversion stats come from Adobe and platform marketing; independent small-merchant volume data was not found.

### Gaps
- No independent data on what share of ChatGPT/AI Mode checkouts go to merchants under, say, $1M revenue.
- Visa TAP and Mastercard Agent Pay merchant onboarding details for small merchants not found.
- No evidence found of consumer-side "agent wallet" products from independent developers.

## 4. Apple iOS 26/27, App Store rule changes, EU DMA, Android: new categories and business models

### Takeaway
Three concrete newly-allowed business models exist as of 2026: (1) US external purchase links at 0% Apple commission (since May 2025, still in force pending the Supreme Court case granted July 2026); (2) cross-developer subscription Bundles/Suites and Group Purchases announced at WWDC26 (details summer/late 2026) — the first time indie apps can sell together; (3) alternative iOS marketplaces in the EU. Apple also opened Siri (built on Google Gemini-derived Apple Foundation Models) to any app via mandatory App Intents, and simultaneously tightened the store (oversaturated-category removals, social-media declaration).

### Cited Findings
- WWDC 2026 (June 8–11): Apple Foundation Models "developed in close collaboration with Google's Gemini"; Apple reportedly licensed a custom ~1.2T-parameter Gemini model at ~$1B/year; new Siri lives in a standalone app and works across apps; App Intents is now the mandatory Siri integration path with SiriKit on a deprecation clock; Xcode 27 ships on-device AI code completion — [TechCrunch WWDC 2026 recap](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/09/wwdc-2026-everything-announced-on-siri-ai-os-27-apple-intelligence-and-more/); [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/apple-unveils-next-generation-of-apple-intelligence-siri-ai-and-more/); [Lushbinary dev guide](https://lushbinary.com/blog/wwdc-2026-announcements-ios-27-siri-developer-guide/)

## 6. AI companions/characters regulation: safer, bounded formats that are open

### Takeaway
SB 243 (effective Jan 1, 2026) plus a 10+-state wave through 2027, the FTC's Sept 2025 6(b) orders, and Character.AI's Nov 25, 2025 under-18 chat ban made open-ended companion chat a legally hostile category for minors and a liability for everyone. Capital has moved to bounded, authored, non-relational formats: kids' interactive stories (Giant, $8M), vertical video series (Holywater, $22M, Jan 2026), writer-to-game tools (Dorian, $14M), AI dramas (c.ai Series, July 2026), and word/duel games (Character.AI, Jan 2025). Character-driven *games* with finite sessions, no memory of the user, and explicit fiction framing are the open, defensible format.

### Cited Findings
- SB 243 signed October 13, 2025, effective January 1, 2026: AI disclosure; for known minors, break reminders at least every three hours and measures against sexual content; enforcement by AG/DAs with civil penalties up to $2,500 per violation; private right of action for the greater of actual damages or $1,000 per violation plus fees — [Jones Walker](https://www.joneswalker.com/en/insights/blogs/ai-law-blog/ai-regulatory-update-californias-sb-243-mandates-companion-ai-safety-and-accoun.html?id=102lq7c); [The Leveraged Years](https://www.theleveragedyears.com/ai-regulation-news/california-sb243-companion-chatbot-safety-private-right-2026); [FPF](https://fpf.org/blog/understanding-the-new-wave-of-chatbot-legislation-california-sb-243-and-beyond/)
- Other chatbot laws and effective dates: Maine LD 1727 (Sept 24, 2025); New Hampshire HB 143 (Jan 1, 2026); Tennessee SB 1580 (July 1, 2026); Colorado HB26-1263 (Aug 12, 2026; operator duties Jan 1, 2027); Connecticut, New York, Oregon, Rhode Island, Washington (Jan 1, 2027); Georgia, Idaho, Iowa, Nebraska (July 1, 2027) — [Fenwick](https://www.fenwick.com/insights/publications/chatbot-compliance-wave-californias-new-law-not-only-one-watch); [MoFo on NY and CA](https://www.mofo.com/resources/insights/251120-new-york-and-california-enact-landmark-ai)
- FTC issued Section 6(b) orders on September 11, 2025 to Character Technologies, Alphabet, Instagram, Meta, OpenAI, Snap, and xAI about companion-chatbot effects on children; 45-day response window — [Nelson Mullins](https://www.nelsonmullins.com/insights/alerts/privacy_and_data_security_alert/all/ftc-announces-children-s-privacy-enforcements-and-launches-ai-chatbot-inquiry); [Fortune, Sept 12, 2025](https://dc.fortune.com/2025/09/12/ftc-inquiry-chatbot-companion-teenagers-kids-children-self-harm)
- Character.AI barred under-18s from open-ended chat from November 25, 2025 (minors keep video/image creation); launched games Speakeasy and War of Words (Jan 2025); launched "c.ai Series" animated short-form AI dramas (July 2026); litigation ongoing in FL, CO, TX, PA — [Wikipedia: Character.ai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Character.ai); [xident on companion AI as age-gated category](https://xident.io/blog/companion-ai-age-verification-sb-243-state-law-wave-2026/)

- a16z: "World models take the spotlight in storytelling" (interactive virtual worlds, "generative Minecraft") — [a16z Big Ideas 2026](https://a16z.com/newsletter/big-ideas-2026-part-1/)

### Inferences
- The open format is "character-driven but not companion": fixed-length, authored scenarios; multiplayer (a character hosts a party game for a friend group); no persistent emotional relationship; explicit game framing. Multiplayer is also what YC's "Multiplayer AI" request points at, and it neatly avoids the "sustained relationship" trigger that companion laws target.
- "No one is here" is partly true at the indie level, but Character.AI (games, Series) and well-funded story apps are moving in. The solo wedge is the party/social-game end (AI game-master for an in-person group), which the story-app funders are not building and which fits "together tech".
- Skeptical note: Fenwick's publication did not reproduce SB 243's exemption text; whether a given game is a "companion chatbot" under SB 243 must be checked against the statute, not summaries. Treat the 3-hour-break and disclosure duties as cheap to comply with regardless.

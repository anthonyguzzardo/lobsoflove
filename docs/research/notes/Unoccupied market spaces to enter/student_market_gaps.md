# Under-served segments in the US college / young-adult (18–26) market, Oct 2026 (excluding anonymous feeds, study tools, dating)

Research date: 2026-10-09. US focus, UC San Diego as home campus. Constraint: no anonymous feeds, no study trackers, no real-money products.

## KQ1. Which campus-focused startups raised, launched, died, or were acquired in 2025–2026, and what gaps did the deaths leave?

### Takeaway
2025–26 was a consolidation year: the two best-funded student socials (Fizz, Sidechat/Yik Yak) stayed alive by pivoting toward commerce and "main chat", Saturn and Geneva were absorbed by Snap and Bumble, Chegg's revenue halved, and Handshake effectively left the student-jobs business to become an AI data vendor. The clearest unoccupied holes are (a) lightweight community/club tooling after Geneva's shutdown, (b) student-facing career/jobs product after Handshake's pivot, and (c) campus textbook/commerce after Chegg's collapse.

### Cited Findings
- Geneva (community/group-chat platform popular with clubs and run-clubs) is being shut down by Bumble and folded into a relaunched "BFF" app; Geneva had generated no revenue as of June 30, 2025; the Groups discovery feature did not ship until Feb 2026 — [TechCrunch, Sept 18 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/18/bumble-bffs-revamped-app-is-here-focusing-on-friend-groups-and-community-building)
- BFF Groups discovery shipped Feb 2026 and is positioned around "local groups tied to events"; Bumble survey: 56% of Gen Z/millennials actively seeking new friendships, 52% want tools to build friend circles — [The Everygirl](https://theeverygirl.com/bumble-for-friends-app/)
- Fizz: 620+ campuses, 27M posts, $41M raised; in Sept 2025 added Gopuff grocery delivery inside the app; claims DAU quadrupled in a month and launched on 250 new campuses — [TechCrunch, Sept 3 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/03/college-social-app-fizz-expands-into-grocery-delivery)
- Fizz hired TikTok's former head of creator monetization as head of product (Feb 2025) to build a recommendation engine, in-app payments for its marketplace, video, and ads — [TechCrunch, Feb 6 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/06/fizz-brings-on-tiktok-alum-to-help-build-out-its-marketplace-and-recommendation-engine/)
- Fizz's "serial failed retiree CEO" stepped down in Oct 2024 and the founder retook the helm — [TechCrunch, Oct 31 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/31/college-social-app-fizzs-serial-failed-retiree-ceo-steps-down-as-founder-takes-helm)
- Sidechat/Yik Yak (merged 2023): CEO Kyle Venn says ~2.2M unique users per semester, ~15% of all US undergraduates — [Northeastern Global News, May 26 2026](https://news.northeastern.edu/2026/05/26/ceo-yik-yak-social-media/)
- Sidechat was the real-time information channel during the Dec 13 2025 Brown University shooting, 15 minutes ahead of the official alert — [Fortune, Jan 8 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/01/08/campus-mass-shootings-sidechat-anonymous-message-board/)
- Sidechat is trying to become "college students' main chat" (group chat, not just anon feed) — [Jordan News / TechCrunch syndication](https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-129/Technology/Sidechat-wants-to-be-college-students-main-chat-17314)
- Snap acquired Saturn (social calendar for HS/college; used by "80% of US high schools") in June 2025; reported price $44M (not confirmed by Snap); app continues standalone for now — [TechCrunch, June 20 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/20/snap-acquires-saturn-a-social-calendar-app-for-high-school-and-college-students); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/snap-inc-acquires-social-calendar-app-saturn-to-deepen-gen%e2%80%91z-engagement-in-u-s-71386.htm)
- Series (Yale, "AI friends" in iMessage making double-opt-in intros) raised $3.1M pre-seed in 14 days, April 2025, led by Parable with Pear VC and others — [Hartford Business](https://hartfordbusiness.com/article/yale-students-raise-31m-in-14-days-for-ai-powered-social-platform/)
- Marriage Pact: still growing; Brown 2026 had 4,177 signups (>50% of on-campus students), up from 3,158 in 2025; 109 campuses since 2017, 314,488 matches — [Brown Daily Herald, Mar 2026](https://www.browndailyherald.com/article/2026/03/how-students-found-lasting-love-through-marriage-pact); Swarthmore 1,179 submissions Dec 2025 — [Swarthmore Phoenix](https://swarthmorephoenix.com/2025/12/11/marriage-pact-goes-for-another-round/)
- Chegg Q2 2026: revenue $51.8M, down 51% YoY; Q3 2026 guidance $43–44M; pivoting to an "employability" platform merging Skills and Busuu — [BusinessWire, Aug 6 2026](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260806916599/en/Chegg-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Earnings); earlier cut 22% then ~45% of staff (388 people) — [AOL/Reuters](https://www.aol.com/articles/chegg-cuts-45-staff-ai-051224772.html)
- Handshake launched Handshake AI in Jan 2025 (data labeling for frontier labs); hit ~$1.1B annualized gross revenue in April 2026, +349% YoY, "after shifting focus from its earlier campus job board product" — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/handshake); acquired Uplimit (AI skilling) June 2026 — [Josh Bersin](https://joshbersin.com/2026/06/handshake-acquires-uplimit-ai-skilling-takes-a-new-direction/)
- Modern Campus (Toronto) acquired Presence (campus engagement platform) — [Tampa Bay Business & Wealth](https://tbbwmag.com/?p=12407); Ready Education's CampusGroups claims 100 institutions switched from Anthology Engage, 7M+ students, 9M event registrations and 6M check-ins in 2025 — [Ready Education 2025 highlights](https://www.readyeducation.com/articles/2025-student-engagement-highlights-a-year-of-strong-growth/)
- Instagram "Schools" launched Aug 26 2025: UNiDAYS-verified school badge, searchable classmate directory filterable by year — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/26/instagrams-new-feature-helps-college-students-connect-with-others-on-campus)
- TikTok "Campus Hub" launched May 1 2026: verified-student group chats of up to 300, college feed; UNiDAYS verification across 6,000+ universities — [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/tiktok-launches-campus-hub-with-college-group-chats-and-personalized-feeds-for-verified-students/)

### Inferences
- Geneva's shutdown removed the one consumer-grade, free, multi-channel "community home" that clubs and run clubs had adopted; BFF is repositioned as a friend-finding product for adults, not a club-ops tool. That leaves clubs back on GroupMe/Discord/Instagram.
- Fizz and Sidechat are both moving toward commerce/"main chat" (Fizz: marketplace + Gopuff; Sidechat: group chat). Their incumbency is in the anonymous feed, not in structured club, sports, or event tooling. Entering their core is foreclosed by the brief anyway.
- Handshake's and Chegg's pivots mean the two biggest "student-facing" edtech brands have effectively stopped investing in the undergraduate experience; their product surface for students is now thin.
- Saturn's acquisition by Snap and Instagram/TikTok campus features show the big platforms are chasing "verified classmates" and schedules; a solo founder should avoid "who is in my class" and "verified campus directory" plays.

### Gaps

- Could not confirm Geneva's college-club usage numbers; TechCrunch piece does not mention clubs.
- No 2025–26 financial health data on Presence post-acquisition or on Uloop (only that it was founded 2007, ~26 employees — [BounceWatch](https://bouncewatch.com/company/uloop)).
- Saturn's $44M price is from secondary reporting, not Snap.

## KQ2. What do student newspapers and r/college-type communities say is missing on big public campuses in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
The loudest, best-documented complaint at big publics is structural isolation (campus size, commuting, college-system silos, no single channel) rather than a missing feature; UCSD's own paper frames "UC Socially Dead" as a self-reinforcing expectations loop that the 2025 basketball run briefly broke.

### Cited Findings
- UCSD Guardian (June 2025): the residential-college system "disincentivizes interaction between students outside their own community, creating cliquey social bubbles"; Seventh College students face "a 20-minute trek" to Geisel/Price Center; construction and parking are "barriers to participation in campus life" for commuters; low expectations create "a feedback loop whereby low expectations discourage participation"; basketball's March Madness run made "school pride visibly surge" — [UCSD Guardian](https://ucsdguardian.org/2025/06/02/uc-socially-dead-on-arrival-how-this-negative-reputation-reinforces-itself/)
- Earlier UCSD Guardian piece on the stigma (2022) — [UCSD Guardian](https://ucsdguardian.org/2022/04/03/the-uc-socially-dead-stigma/)
- UCSD Common Data Set 2024–25: 48% of undergrads live in university housing, 52% off campus/commute; ~10% of men and 10% of women in Greek life — [UCSD IR CDS section F](https://ir.ucsd.edu/stats/undergrad/F2.pdf)
- Nearly half of college students screen positive for loneliness (NCHA Fall 2024: 48.2%), yet 82% say they find community on campus, not online — [flytedesk](https://flytedesk.com/insights/students-belonging-community); [NASPA](https://www.naspa.org/blog/examining-college-student-loneliness-and-what-student-affairs-educators-can-do-about-it)
- Commuters (Univ. of Dayton, Fall 2025, n=60) "feel disconnected from the campus community across a variety of dimensions" and use commuter resources at low rates — [Univ. of Dayton Stander Symposium](https://ecommons.udayton.edu/stander_posters/4126); commuters are ~40% of undergrads at George Fox — [George Fox survey](https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/ZMLFB9G)
- Transfer students report classroom isolation; "I commute, so it's hard for me to make friends and feel connected" — [JMU transfer report](https://jmu.edu/pair/ads/survey_research/transfer-survey/trn-report.pdf)
- Club leaders juggle GroupMe, Discord, email, Instagram, Slack, iMessage simultaneously and find it "frustrating to keep track of it all"; Discord servers go unchecked by non-Discord users; Instagram is bad for documents — [GSU student blog](https://sites.gsu.edu/vpatel86/?p=165); [Daily Princetonian](https://dailyprincetonian.com/article/2020/10/princeton-student-groups-use-discord-for-community)
- Student-built event/club discovery apps keep appearing (CircleU, Arch live event map, Circula map, CampusGrids at Ottawa), which is itself evidence of the recurring unmet need — [CircleU App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ng/app/circleu/id6753159804); [Arch](https://mwm.ai/apps/arch-college/6773485523); [CampusGrids](https://thefulcrum.ca/news/campusgrids-brings-clubs-and-students-together/)
- UCSD's only notable student-facing app coverage in the Guardian's "apps" tag is Willo, a wellness app (Oct 2024) — [UCSD Guardian apps tag](https://ucsdguardian.org/tag/apps/)
- UCSD has a Triton-specific commuter/transfer problem documented in its own transfer-survey program — [UCSD Transfer Student end-of-quarter survey](https://transferstudents.ucsd.edu/_files/for-faculty-page/2023-end-of-first-quarter-survey.pdf)

### Inferences
- At UCSD the "can't find each other" problem is physical (1,000 acres, eight colleges, 52% off-campus) more than informational. Products that create reasons to cross college lines (inter-college competition, campus-wide rituals) attack the root cause better than another event listing.
- The 82%-find-community-on-campus stat argues for products that drive in-person co-presence, not more online feeds.
- The repeated student-built "event map" apps die with their founders (no business model, no distribution) — evidence of demand and of a graveyard; a wedge needs a sharper hook than "all events in one place."

### Gaps
- Did not find a 2025–26 r/UCSD or r/college thread with explicit "I wish there was an app for X" language via web search (Reddit content is poorly indexed); recommend direct Reddit search.
- No 2025–26 student-newspaper op-ed specifically about event-discovery failure was found.

## KQ3. Is there a student-native product for college-vs-college / house-cup competition, campus leagues, or athletics attendance/rewards, and how healthy are they?

### Takeaway
Athletics rewards is served only by B2B check-in-for-points vendors sold to athletic departments (SuperFan, FanX/Snap! Raise), house-cup competition exists only as hand-run honors-college programs, and intramural ops runs on IMLeagues, which students openly call bad. There is no student-native, fun-first, cross-campus competition product.

### Cited Findings
- SuperFan: athletic-department-sold check-in/points app (Keystone College 2021; Amherst launched April 2024, rewards are merch) — [WC Examiner](https://www.wcexaminer.com/2021/07/20/keystone-to-enhance-fan-experience); [Amherst Student](https://amherststudent.com/article/superfan-app-aims-to-boost-student-engagement/)
- FanX Rewards (Snap! Raise) markets rewards+analytics to schools — [Snap! Raise](https://snapraise.com/blog/fanx-rewards-analytics/)
- UMD University Honors runs a Hogwarts-style House Cup with points for wellness, campus engagement and community events, standings emailed weekly; House Eltanin won 2025 — [Maryland Today](https://today.umd.edu/university-honors-takes-lesson-from-hogwarts); [UMD Honors](https://universityhonors.umd.edu/?p=939)
- RPTU (Germany) ran a 4-week "Campus Games" app assigning players to houses by campus, May 4–28 2026 — [RPTU](https://zsgw.rptu.de/en/campusplus/games/campusgames/das-spiel/spielanleitung)
- IMLeagues is used by 1,000+ universities; Drexel's paper (May 2025) calls it "confusing navigation, outdated design and questionable functionality," with login lists showing 2012 leagues — [The Triangle](https://cms.thetriangle.org/sports/imleagues-website-bad-intramural-sports/)
- UCSD athletics: LionTree Arena capacity 4,000; 2025 Big West champions / NCAA tournament; Nov 8 2025 replica-banner giveaway; Fresno State homecoming (2026–27) gives 1,000 students free t-shirts; UCI "Blue-Gold rivalry" game Jan 2026 — [UCSD Tritons](https://ucsdtritons.com/news/2026/9/1/mens-basketball-tritons-to-host-fresno-state-homecoming-weekend.aspx); [UCSD Tritons](https://ucsdtritons.com/news/2026/1/23/mens-basketball-blue-gold-rivalry-reignites-as-uci-comes-to-la-jolla-saturday.aspx); [UCSD Tritons](https://ucsdtritons.com/news/2025/8/28/mens-basketball-mens-hoops-announces-non-conference-schedule.aspx)
- Esports clubs growing: NACE 260+ member institutions; 2026 convention drew 200+ registrants; UGA's esports club nearly doubled in 3 years — [Seton Hall](https://www.shu.edu/esports/news/shu-esports-nace-announcement.html); [UNF](https://www.unf.edu/newsroom/2026/07/Esports-NACE-National-Convention.html); [UGA Grady](https://grady.uga.edu/news/esports-at-uga-seeks-to-build-financial-support/)

### Inferences
- The attendance-rewards category is a top-down athletic-department sale (SuperFan/FanX), incompatible with a solo founder who dislikes cold outreach. The bottom-up version, a student-run "Triton Tide" style fan club with its own points and bragging rights, has no software.
- UCSD's eight residential colleges are a ready-made house system; no one has turned that into a persistent, app-mediated inter-college competition (Muir vs Revelle vs Seventh...) spanning intramurals, attendance, trivia, esports. The UMD House Cup proves the format works but is hand-run by staff.
- Intramurals are a captive, recurring, emotionally-invested audience with a hated incumbent (IMLeagues) — but replacing it is a rec-department sale. The wedge is the social layer on top (team chat, standings, highlight sharing), not the registration backend.

### Gaps
- No 2025–26 data on FanRally or Spectrum found; cannot assess health.
- No published UCSD student-attendance figures or Triton Tide membership numbers were found.
- No evidence of a student-native cross-campus competition product in the US; absence of evidence, not confirmed absence.

## KQ4. Student org tooling: what do club leaders use in 2026 and what breaks?

### Takeaway
Clubs run on a GroupMe/Discord/Instagram/Google Forms/Linktree stack plus a university-mandated engagement platform (CampusGroups, Engage, Presence); the breakage is fragmentation and the gap between the "official" admin system and where members actually talk. Geneva's death removed the consumer-grade alternative.

### Cited Findings
- Fragmentation complaints: GroupMe + Discord + email with no all-in-one; leaders using six platforms at once — [GSU](https://sites.gsu.edu/vpatel86/?p=165)
- Discord downsides for clubs: non-users won't check the server; Instagram poor for documents — [Daily Princetonian](https://dailyprincetonian.com/article/2020/10/princeton-student-groups-use-discord-for-community)
- Student-side club apps appear and vanish (Syracuse "C!ub" app) — [Syracuse Libraries](https://library.syracuse.edu/blog/cub-app-connects-students-with-clubs/)
- CampusGroups (institutional) grew: 2.3M new users (+42%), 9M event registrations, 6M check-ins, 154M emails sent by admins/leaders in 2025; AI dashboards for 2026; LSU cites 90% retention for involved students vs 74% — [Ready Education](https://www.readyeducation.com/articles/2025-student-engagement-highlights-a-year-of-strong-growth/)
- Geneva, used for group communities, shut down into Bumble BFF; discovery arrived Feb 2026 — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/18/bumble-bffs-revamped-app-is-here-focusing-on-friend-groups-and-community-building)
- Greek chapters have their own paid ops stack (GreekTrack, OmegaFi/OmegaOne/OmegaRecruit: dues, rosters, events, SMS) — [Capterra GreekTrack](https://www.capterra.com/p/161167/GreekTrack/); [Dealroom OmegaFi](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/omegafi); [Snowball dues list](https://snowballfundraising.com/top-fraternity-dues-collection-services/)
- Greek participation rising in 2025–26: UVA sororities hit a record 3,082 members (Spring 2026); JMU record recruitment; Utah recruitment +79% since 2020 (+35% 2024→25); WKU membership up despite enrollment down — [Cavalier Daily](https://www.cavalierdaily.com/article/2026/02/fraternity-and-sorority-recruitment-sees-influx-in-registration); [The Breeze](https://www.breezejmu.org/news/2025-marks-highest-fsl-recruitment-levels-from-last-few-years/article_e795d212-b56d-4ef2-9073-46ca4e0f42d4.html); [Utah FSL](https://studentaffairs.utah.edu/blog/posts/2026/july/fsl_recruitment.php); [WKU report](https://www.wku.edu/wkugreeks/reports/2025-2026greek_annual_report.docx)
- 154M admin emails in one year is the institutional channel's own admission that it reaches students via email, not where they live — [Ready Education](https://www.readyeducation.com/articles/2025-student-engagement-highlights-a-year-of-strong-growth/)

### Inferences
- The institutional layer (CampusGroups/Engage) is healthy and consolidating; do not compete with it. The open space is the member-facing social layer (attendance streaks, rivalries, "who's coming", fun), which clubs patch together with GroupMe polls.
- Greek life is growing again and already pays for software; but its ops vendors are dues/compliance tools. The fun layer (inter-chapter competition, philanthropy leaderboards, Greek Week) is unserved and buyer is a student officer, not an administrator.
- A solo founder can enter via one club they are already in (no cold outreach), with a tool that leaders would show other leaders.

### Gaps
- No 2026-specific survey of club-leader tool usage found; evidence is anecdotal from student blogs/papers.
- No data on Linktree/Google Forms penetration in clubs.

## KQ5. International students: with 2025–2026 visa turbulence, what services are missing?

### Takeaway
International enrollment is projected to fall 9.5% (~111,000 students) in Fall 2026 under a 39-country travel ban, visa appointment shortages, and the new fixed "Admit-Until-Date" rule; the documented pain is policy/visa and housing scams, while the day-to-day belonging gap for those who do arrive is under-served and now shrinking as a market.

### Cited Findings
- NAFSA (Aug 11 2026): 9.5% decline, up to 111,000 fewer students, $3.4B lost spending, 39,000+ jobs; travel ban EO Dec 16 2025 on 39 countries with no student exception; limited visa appointments in India, China, Europe; Duration of Status replaced by fixed Admit-Until-Date effective Sept 15 2026; State Dept prioritized FIFA World Cup visas through July 19, disrupting the May–Aug student-visa peak; international doctoral applications down 21% — [NAFSA](https://www.nafsa.org/about/about-nafsa/us-economy-stands-lose-34-billion-precipitous-drop-international-students)
- Housing scams target students: UIUC student asked for $800 via Zelle via the "UIUC Housing Sublet & Roommates" Facebook group; roughly half of rental scams begin with a fake Facebook ad — [UIUC Police](https://www.police.illinois.edu/author/dps/page/7/); [uni2study](https://www.uni2study.com/articles/off-campus-housing-scams-targeting-students); [WFLX, Mar 2026](https://www.wflx.com/2026/03/17/college-students-targeted-off-campus-subletting-scams)

### Inferences
- International students are a shrinking, higher-regulatory-risk segment in 2026; banking/visa products require compliance a solo founder cannot carry. Better framed as a sub-audience of belonging/housing products than a standalone target.
- Verified, campus-scoped sublet/roommate matching (where identity is .edu-verified) directly addresses the scam vector that hits international students hardest.

### Gaps

- No UCSD-specific international enrollment change for Fall 2026 found.

## KQ6. Secondhand / campus commerce: what happened to campus marketplaces, and is there a gap?

### Takeaway
Campus commerce is served by Facebook groups (scam-prone), Fizz's in-app marketplace, Depop/Marketplace generally, and a steady churn of single-campus student apps (TigerSwap, Dibs!, Campus Swap); Chegg's collapse removed the textbook-rental default. The persistent gap is trusted, time-boxed, campus-scoped exchanges (move-out, sublets, tickets).

### Cited Findings
- TigerSwap (Princeton, May 2025): replaced "HoagieMail" email slideshows for selling clothes; 408 downloads at writing; category filters, DMs, pickup spots — [Daily Princetonian](https://www.dailyprincetonian.com/article/2025/05/princeton-prospect-lifestyle-tigerswap-fashion-clothes-move-out-app-no-more-slideshows)
- Campus Swap (Davidson): picks up move-out items, stores and sells them; $15 pickup for 50% of profit or free pickup for 20% — [Davidson Hurt Hub](https://hurthub.davidson.edu/2026/03/31/)
- Dibs! (Northeastern Oakland) student marketplace for clothes/dorm furnishings — [Northeastern News](https://news.northeastern.edu/?p=260612)
- Fizz runs a peer-to-peer marketplace and is adding in-app payments and Gopuff delivery — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/03/college-social-app-fizz-expands-into-grocery-delivery)
- Sublet market described as "confusing, inefficient, and unsafe," transacted on social media and word of mouth; Dormsy (Wharton business plan) and Homey (swipe-first sublease marketplace) are attempts — [Dormsy BP](https://fisher.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Dormsy_BP.pdf); [Homey on Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/p/homey-6/homey-7)
- Chegg Q2 2026 revenue -51% YoY; company reorienting to "employability" — [BusinessWire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260806916599/en/Chegg-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Earnings)
- Uloop (founded 2007, ~26 employees) still lists textbooks, tickets, furniture, housing — [BounceWatch](https://bouncewatch.com/company/uloop)

### Inferences
- Move-out is the single highest-intent, most time-boxed commerce moment on campus (June at UCSD), with real waste and real scams; the student-app graveyard here is about distribution, not demand.
- Fizz now owns "generic campus marketplace"; competing head-on is unwise. Niches Fizz won't do well: verified sublets/roommates, dorm move-out logistics, ticket swaps for sold-out student sections.
- Textbooks are not a good solo wedge: demand is shrinking (AI, OER, inclusive-access) and Chegg's collapse was caused by the same forces.

## KQ7. What tactics are 2026 campus launches using, and does that foreclose anything?

### Takeaway
Big platforms are launching "verified classmates" layers (Instagram Schools Aug 2025, TikTok Campus Hub May 2026, both via UNiDAYS; Snap owns Saturn), and Fizz/Sidechat launch by campus with ambassadors and giveaways. This forecloses generic campus directories, class-schedule social, and anonymous feeds, but not structured, fun, cross-group competition or ops layers.

### Cited Findings
- Instagram Schools: UNiDAYS-verified badge, classmate directory filterable by year, launched Aug 26 2025 — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/26/instagrams-new-feature-helps-college-students-connect-with-others-on-campus)
- TikTok Campus Hub: 300-person verified group chats + college feed, May 1 2026, 6,000+ universities, explicitly aimed at Discord/Messenger/Instagram use cases like "plan reunions, keep conversations going over the summer" — [Shopifreaks](https://www.shopifreaks.com/tiktok-launches-campus-hub-with-college-group-chats-and-personalized-feeds-for-verified-students/)
- Snap/Saturn: schedule-based social for HS/college — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/20/snap-acquires-saturn-a-social-calendar-app-for-high-school-and-college-students)
- Fizz launch playbook: campus-by-campus, 250 new campuses in one push, record registrations 12 days running — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/03/college-social-app-fizz-expands-into-grocery-delivery)
- Marriage Pact playbook: once-a-year campus-wide ritual, >50% participation at Brown, spreads via student reps — [Brown Daily Herald](https://www.browndailyherald.com/article/2026/03/how-students-found-lasting-love-through-marriage-pact)
- Sidechat/Yik Yak ran a 100,000-student Valentine's compatibility survey Feb 2026 (ritual tactic) — [MEXC news aggregate](https://www.mexc.com/news/643187)
- Bumble BFF Groups discovery (Feb 2026) ties groups to local events — [The Everygirl](https://theeverygirl.com/bumble-for-friends-app/)

### Inferences
- The "seasonal campus-wide ritual" (Marriage Pact, Valentine's surveys, March Madness) is the most efficient distribution tactic for a solo founder: one moment, one campus, a reason for everyone to participate, no cold outreach. Rituals built around rivalry (inter-college, UCSD vs UCI) are open.
- UNiDAYS-verified identity is now commodity infrastructure inside Instagram/TikTok; a new app should use .edu verification as table stakes but not as the value prop.
- Platforms will not build campus-specific competition mechanics, standings, or house identities; those are too niche for them and are where a solo product can own the loop.

### Gaps
- No post-launch usage data for Instagram Schools or TikTok Campus Hub found.
- No reporting on whether Snap has integrated Saturn into Snapchat by Oct 2026.

## KQ8. Segment-by-segment scorecard (demand, incumbents, why open, solo-founder wedge)

### Takeaway
Ranking by (demand evidence) x (weak incumbents) x (fit with a fun/belonging, no-cold-outreach solo founder), the strongest spaces are: inter-college / inter-group competition on big campuses, club member-facing social layer post-Geneva, intramural/club-sports social layer, student fan-club tooling, campus move-out/sublet trust layer, and Greek/faith/esports community rituals. Weakest fits: international-student fintech, textbooks, parents, gig work, alumni networks.

### Cited Findings
- Belonging: ~48% of students screen lonely; 82% find community on campus not online; first-gen 30% more likely to feel isolated first year (Gallup-Lumina 2024, as cited by NASPA) — [flytedesk](https://flytedesk.com/insights/students-belonging-community); [NASPA](https://www.naspa.org/blog/examining-college-student-loneliness-and-what-student-affairs-educators-can-do-about-it)
- Involvement drives retention (LSU 90% vs 74%) — [Ready Education](https://www.readyeducation.com/articles/2025-student-engagement-highlights-a-year-of-strong-growth/)
- Faith: Harris Poll (Sept 2026) puts Gen Z among the most religious generations, 33% identify as religious vs 19% of millennials, 49% attended a recent service — [Daily Signal](https://www.dailysignal.com/2026/09/24/poll-shows-spiritual-revival-gen-z/); Cru operates on 2,088 campuses and reports rising conversion rates; 200+ baptized at Auburn — [College Fix (Feb 2024)](https://www.thecollegefix.com/christian-campus-ministries-report-spike-in-interest-as-lost-gen-z-seeks-meaning-guidance/); ASU received 52 students into the Catholic Church Fall 2025 — [Aleteia](https://aleteia.org/2025/12/06/confessions-and-candlelight-faith-grows-on-campus/); K-State has 25 registered religious orgs — [K-State Collegian](https://kstatecollegian.com/2025/11/06/gen-z-flocks-to-church/)
- Music/DJ: "revived DJ culture" at UIUC, "there are DJs everywhere now"; Gen Z live-music attendance +30% vs pre-pandemic (Eventbrite 2024) with electronic leading 18–24 — [Daily Illini, May 2025](https://dailyillini.com/life_and_culture-stories/arts-and-entertainment/2025/05/02/revived-dj-culture-around-campus); Dartmouth's Booth DJ collective supplies Greek houses — [The Dartmouth](https://thedartmouth.com/article/2022/04/student-spotlight-booth-brings-djing-to-campus-events)
- Early career: recent-grad unemployment 5.7% (Q4 2025) and 5.6% (Mar 2026, NY Fed); junior postings -7% — [Scripps News](https://www.scrippsnews.com/politics/economy/class-of-2026-faces-tough-job-market-and-ai-concerns-as-graduation-season-approaches); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/select/class-of-2026-hiring-stats-and-ai-trends/)
- Parents: CampusESP serves 500+ institutions, 15M parents; 81% of parents expect weekly communication — [CampusESP](https://campusesp.com)
- On-campus gig: Dormzi (laundry/tutoring/errands between students), Gigsurf (students for local businesses), Campus Job ($7.8M Series A, older) — [cr80news](https://cr80news.com/news-item/a-gig-economy-designed-for-the-college-campus/?tag=mobile); [Gigsurf](https://startupsummer.gatech.edu/company/gigsurf); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1150819)
- Greek growth (see KQ4) and 10% Greek at UCSD — [UCSD CDS](https://ir.ucsd.edu/stats/undergrad/F2.pdf)
- Esports club growth (see KQ3).

### Inferences (scorecard)
| Segment | Demand evidence | Incumbents (health) | Why open | Solo-founder wedge (fun/belonging, no cold outreach) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Inter-college / house-cup competition on big campuses | UCSD loneliness + 8-college silos; UMD House Cup works; basketball run united campus | None student-native; UMD/RPTU hand-run; SuperFan is dept-sold | Platforms won't build campus-specific standings; departments lack product | Seasonal "Triton Cup": Muir vs Revelle etc. earning points from IM wins, game attendance check-ins, trivia nights; launch inside one college council |
| Club member-facing social layer | Leaders juggle 5–6 tools; Geneva gone; CampusGroups is admin email | GroupMe/Discord (healthy but generic); CampusGroups (healthy, B2B) | Consumer club-community product vacated Sept 2025 | Start with the founder's own clubs: attendance streaks, "who's going", club-vs-club challenges; spreads leader-to-leader |
| Intramural / club sports social layer | 1,000+ schools on IMLeagues; students call it bad | IMLeagues (entrenched, disliked) | Rec depts own registration but not the social layer | Team chat + standings + highlights + rivalry feed that imports public IMLeagues schedules; no sale needed |
| Student fan club / attendance bragging rights | UCSD D1 since 2020, 2025 NCAA; giveaways show dept wants students there | SuperFan/FanX (B2B) | Bottom-up fan clubs have no tooling; non-money rewards (status, streaks) allowed | "Triton Tide" streak app: check in, section leaderboards per college, away-game watch parties |
| Move-out / sublet trust layer | Scams on FB groups; TigerSwap, Campus Swap, Dibs! appearing 2025–26 | Facebook groups (scam-prone), Fizz marketplace (growing) | Fizz is generic; nobody does verified, time-boxed move-out/sublet | .edu-verified June move-out + summer sublet board scoped to UCSD; seasonal ritual |
| Greek life fun layer | Record recruitment 2025–26 | OmegaFi/GreekTrack (dues/compliance) | No inter-chapter competition / Greek Week product | Greek Week leaderboard + philanthropy challenges; buyer is a student officer |
| Faith groups | Gen Z religiosity rising; Cru on 2,088 campuses | GroupMe, church apps (Planning Center) | Campus ministries are communities, not clubs; small-group matching unserved | Small-group finder + rhythm (weekly check-in, meals) for campus ministries |
| Esports / gaming clubs | NACE 260+ schools, clubs doubling | Discord (dominant) | Discord does not do campus-scoped ladders or inter-college cups | Inter-college gaming cup inside the house-cup product |
| Music / DJ scene | DJ culture reviving; Gen Z live attendance up | Instagram, Partiful, RA | No campus-scoped "who's playing tonight" with student-only RSVP | Student-show listings + DJ booking for college councils / Greek houses |
| First-gen / transfer / commuter belonging | 30% more isolation; commuters disconnected | University programs (offices, not products) | Programs are staff-run; no peer product | Commuter "same-schedule crew" matching by arrival block; piggyback on house-cup |
| International students | Enrollment -9.5% F26; scams | Universities, NAFSA | Shrinking market, regulatory load | Sub-audience of sublet/trust product, not standalone |
| Textbooks | Chegg -51% | Amazon, OER, inclusive access | Demand itself is shrinking | Avoid |
| Parents | 81% want weekly comms | CampusESP (healthy, 500+ schools) | B2B incumbent strong | Avoid |
| Campus gig work | Dormzi, Gigsurf | Handshake (left), Campus Job | Real-money, labor-law exposure | Avoid per constraints |
| Alumni / early career | 5.6–5.7% grad unemployment | LinkedIn, Handshake (pivoted) | Handshake vacuum is real but productivity-shaped | Avoid for this founder profile |

### Gaps
- Graduation/commencement: no 2025–26 sources found on products or pain points beyond the job-market stats.
- Alumni/early-career networks: no evidence found of a 2025–26 student-native product filling Handshake's gap.
- No quantitative data on esports club or DJ scene at UCSD specifically.
- First-gen Gallup-Lumina stat is cited secondhand (NASPA); verify against the original report before quoting in the final report.

# Unoccupied / under-served spaces in consumer social and entertainment apps (as of October 2026)

Scope note: Findings dated 2025–2026 unless marked "(background)". Research done 2026-10-09. The founder-fit lens (solo, Expo/React Native + AI, San Diego / UC San Diego, fun/belonging/charity; no real-money gambling, no productivity/study tools, no anonymous feeds) is applied in the final synthesis section but candidates were not filtered out on fit.

## Key Question 1: What consumer social apps launched, blew up, or died in 2025–2026, and what do the survivors share?

### Takeaway
2025–26 was a graveyard for big-budget, feed-shaped or novelty social: Sora (OpenAI), Rec Room, Digg's reboot, X Communities, Spoony and Niantic's Peridot all shut down, and Bluesky's actives halved. What survived or grew were products with a concrete, repeated real-world job: private friend-group sharing (Locket, Yope, Retro), fitness-as-social (Strava), event/IRL coordination (Partiful, Timeleft, 222), and campus-locked networks (Fizz) — not new public feeds.

### Inferences
- Pattern of the dead: public/open graph + heavy infra cost (Sora video gen, Rec Room VR, Digg front page) + bot/spam exposure (Digg, X Communities). Anything with an open "front page" is now a bot-defense business first.
- Pattern of the survivors: small closed graphs (Locket, Yope, Retro), a recurring physical ritual (Strava runs, Timeleft dinners), and a cheap, repeatable loop (widget photo, weekly recap). Several monetize with a ~$4–10/mo subscription and no ads (Locket, Retro, Finch, Strava).
- "AI-native social" is a funded thesis (Series, Eigen, Yope's recaps) but the AI-generated-content feed variant (Sora) failed outright; the AI-as-matchmaker/organizer variant is still unproven at scale and unshipped (Eigen).
- The bar for a solo founder is not "beat Instagram"; it is "own a ritual inside one closed group," which is exactly the shape of Locket/Retro — both of which were effectively small teams at launch.

## Key Question 2: Meta Arena, Kalshi/Polymarket mainstreaming — what adjacent spaces are open (verification/integrity, private-group markets, non-money stakes, charity stakes)?

### Takeaway
Real-money prediction markets exploded ($50B combined 2025 volume, 2026 tripling) and are now under Congressional insider-trading probes; Meta's Arena is an internal, play-money, AI-generated-markets app targeting 18–34 and 100M MAU but is unconfirmed and may never ship. The non-money/friend-group layer is populated only by tiny indie apps and Manifold, which is the one validated play-money community; private-group, charity-stake and integrity/verification layers have no notable startups.

### Cited Findings
- Meta Arena: internal name "Arena"; play-money with daily allotments; target 18–34, goal of at least 100M MAU; Llama generates market questions from trending topics, personalizes recommendations and resolves markets "in near real-time"; social layer = discussion/debate, leaderboards, integration into FB/IG/Messenger/WhatsApp Stories and Reels; VP Ime Archibong framed it as "betting as conversation"; "in internal testing; may never release publicly"; Meta has not ruled out real money later; reported by NYT June 2026 and NPR — [Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/meta-prediction-market-app); [NPR, Jun 24 2026](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/24/nx-s1-5869486/meta-prediction-market-app-ai)
- Kalshi + Polymarket processed a combined ~$50B in trades in 2025; 2026 volume had already tripled the prior year by June — [Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/meta-prediction-market-app)
- Integrity crisis: Mar 23 2026 both platforms announced insider-trading curbs incl. employment verification before trading corporate/national-security events; Kalshi blocks politicians/athletes from relevant markets; Polymarket codified three prohibited-insider categories — [Venable, Apr 2026](https://www.venable.com/insights/publications/2026/04/kalshi-and-polymarket-move-to-curb-insider); Kalshi now collects users' job details — [Al Jazeera, Jun 10 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/6/10/prediction-platform-kalshi-to-collect-job-details-to-combat-insider-trading)
- DOJ charged a US special-forces soldier for betting on the Maduro capture on Polymarket before the operation, and a Google engineer for trading on internal search info — [Al Jazeera, Jun 10 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/6/10/prediction-platform-kalshi-to-collect-job-details-to-combat-insider-trading)
- House Oversight Chair Comer launched an insider-trading investigation into Kalshi and Polymarket (May 22 2026); Democrats urged CFTC to rein in sports-event contracts (Apr 30 2026) — [CNBC, May 22 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/22/kalshi-polymarket-comer-insider-trading-probe-congress.html); [CNBC, Apr 30 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/30/congress-kalshi-polymarket-prediction-markets-cftc.html); [House Oversight release](https://oversight.house.gov/release/comer-launches-investigation-into-insider-trading-on-prediction-market-platforms/)
- Manifold (founded Dec 2021): play-money "Mana," anyone can create a market, comments/follows/leaderboards/Discord; the only outbound path for Mana is charity donation at M100 per $1 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manifold_(prediction_market)); reviewers call it "the leading play-money prediction market in 2026" — [Tech-Insider review](https://tech-insider.org/prediction-markets/platforms/manifold-markets-review/)
- Kalshi has "minimal community/social features within the app" per a 2026 comparison — [AlphaScope](https://www.alphascope.app/blog/best-prediction-market-app)
- Indie friend-group, no-money prediction apps exist but are tiny/new: "Called It: Predict Friends" (iOS, "just launched," virtual Cred, sports/crypto/polls/daily debates) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/called-it-predict-friends/id6762042980); BeTeam (private prediction leagues for 75+ soccer competitions, World Cup 2026 pools) — [BeTeam](https://beteamapp.com/); Bernibets ("bragging rights are the ultimate reward") — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.bernikins.bets&hl=en); FRR (coins-only bets with friends) — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6758548976); FriendlyBet (open-source World Cup pool) — [friendlybet.live](https://friendlybet.live/)

### Inferences
- Meta's own framing ("betting as conversation," 18–34, leaderboards) validates that the social/play-money layer is the open consumer prize; but Meta's version is feed-integrated and trending-topic-driven, i.e. public and impersonal. A private-group version (markets only your friend group/dorm/club can see, about your own lives and local events) is not what Arena is building and is not what Manifold (public, forecaster-nerd) is.
- Charity stakes are only a faint afterthought at Manifold (Mana to charity). No one has built "we each stake $5, loser's money goes to the winner's charity" or "predict to donate" as a product; this is real-money-adjacent but not gambling if stakes are donations, though legal review is needed.
- Verification/integrity is a B2B/regtech opening (employment attestation, insider screening), well outside consumer social and crowded by legal pressure; not a solo-consumer play.
- Risk: if Arena ships, it will instantly own the generic play-money mass market via Instagram distribution. A solo founder should only enter with a wedge Meta won't do: closed groups, local/campus, charity, or non-event stakes (dares, chores, group goals).

### Gaps
- No confirmation Arena has launched or been cancelled as of Oct 2026; no public user data.
- No usage numbers for Called It, Bernibets, FRR or BeTeam; could not establish whether any has traction.
- Did not find any startup explicitly building "charity-stake" prediction or social betting in 2025–26; absence of evidence, not evidence of absence.
- Legal status of donation-staked predictions in California was not researched.

## Key Question 3: Which IRL coordination / friend-group / third-place apps exist in 2026, and what remains unserved for 18–30 year olds?

### Takeaway
The category is busy at the "meet strangers" end (Timeleft, 222, Pie, Bumble BFF, Meet5, Wyzr, Washed Up, Clyx, Mmotion) and at the "invite people you know" end (Partiful), with Strava clubs swallowing the fitness-social slice; collectively the friendship apps did ~$16M US consumer spend and 4.3M downloads in 2025. What is thin is the middle: keeping an existing friend group doing things together week after week, cheap/free and tailored to 18–24s, plus anything that works for men.

### Cited Findings
- TechCrunch Apr 2026 roundup: "over a dozen local-focused friendship apps" generated ~$16M US consumer spending and ~4.3M downloads in 2025 (Appfigures). Apps: 222 (iOS only, personality-matched outings, $22.22 curation fee or subscription), Bumble BFF (standalone since 2023, redesigned around group meetups), Clyx (Miami/London, group event discovery with Ticketmaster/TikTok data), Les Amís (women/LGBTQ+, $55–70 per event), Meetup, Meet5 (40+, ~777K US downloads 2025), Pie (AI quiz, groups of six; Austin/Chicago/SF), Timeleft (Wednesday 7pm stranger dinners), Washed Up (LA, launched early 2026, event discovery + group planning), Wyzr Friends (40+), Mmotion (NYC, location-sharing interest groups, application-gated), Synchrony (Mar 2026, neurodivergent adults, $44.99/mo) — [TechCrunch, Apr 5 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/)
- Partiful: $20M Series A1 Nov 2022 led by a16z, ~$27.3M total raised, $140M last valuation; a reported fresh round at $400M was disputed by a spokesperson; free to use — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/partiful/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partiful)
- Timeleft: one seed round (2021, FJ Labs, undisclosed) per Tracxn — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/timeleft/__aDvq69-tMF73H6WNRLGae_DzNk5Jz5rVxD_kw2Poqpg/funding-and-investors)
- Strava: running-club creation up 3.5x YoY through late 2025; 1M+ clubs; more than half of Gen Z plan to use Strava more in 2026 while using Instagram/TikTok the same or less; 72% of Gen Z join run clubs to socialize — [Running Lookout on Strava 2026 community report](https://runninglookout.com/news/strava-2026-community-report-running-clubs-3-5x-growth-gen-z/); [SGI Europe on Strava Gen Z study](https://www.sgieurope.com/consumer/gen-z-chooses-fitness-over-social-media-strava-study-reveals/118471.article); 2026 London Marathon applications +31% to 1.1M; Gen Z seeking alcohol-free social activities — [TechCrunch, Oct 12 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/12/strava-eyes-ipo-as-gen-z-trades-dating-apps-for-running-clubs/)
- Club-organizer tooling (RSVPs, attendance, organizer portal) is being filled by small vendors such as SportLync, which positions itself as the run-club management layer while conceding Strava owns the activity feed — [SportLync (vendor)](https://sportlync.com/blog/best-run-club-apps-2026)
- Third-place narrative: malls closing, "hostile" public space, physical third places "fading away or becoming too expensive" for Gen Z; wellness clubs (saunas, breathwork) rising as paid third places — [Yara Circle (secondary)](https://www.yaracircle.com/blog/community/third-place-crisis-gen-z-nowhere-to-make-friends); [Pedestrian.tv on Scape Gen Z Wellbeing Index](https://www.pedestrian.tv/culture/scape-gen-z-wellbeing-index-loneliness-epidemic/)
- Pew (Apr 15 2026): 60% of teens spend time with friends online daily or nearly daily; only 24% do so in person that often — [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/04/15/teens-experiences-on-tiktok-instagram-and-snapchat/)
- Eigen's $15M Benchmark seed is explicitly for "synchronous experiences that bind people together" with real friends — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/16/exclusive-eigen-seed-round-benchmark-capital-ben-silbermann-mutual-friend/)
- Background (2024–25): a16z Speedrun co-led a $12M seed for Sweatpals (fitness-community events) in Oct 2024 and invested in Plots in Jan 2025 — [startupfundraising.com (secondary)](https://startupfundraising.com/research/consumer-social-in-san-francisco-ca/state-of-funding-2026)

### Inferences
- Stranger-matching dinners/outings are commoditized and city-launch-gated (each app lists 2–5 cities; most charge $20–70 per event); a solo founder cannot out-operate Timeleft/222 ops-heavy models.
- Unserved for 18–30: (a) existing-group recurring rituals (weekly "what are we doing" with commitment mechanics) — Partiful is one-off-event-shaped, group chats are the incumbent; (b) free/cheap options — the stranger apps price for 25–40 professionals; (c) men — every friendship app in the TechCrunch list targets women, LGBTQ+, 40+, neurodivergent, or is gender-neutral; none is designed around male friendship despite the Gallup data (Q5); (d) campus-local, non-anonymous, non-toxic coordination — Fizz/Sidechat own campus attention but are anonymous and under bans.
- The run-club boom shows the winning format is activity-first, recurring, sober, and free; an app that makes it trivial to start and keep a micro-club (5–15 people, any activity) with attendance streaks is the Strava-club pattern generalized beyond running. Risk: Strava clubs + group chat already cover running; the opening is non-fitness activities (board games, surf, study-free hangs, volunteering).

## Key Question 4: Gaps in daily-ritual games (Wordle-shaped, NYT Games, Puzzmo) and collectible/character-driven social (Tamagotchi-likes, Pokémon-Go-shaped, Finch)?

### Takeaway
Daily word/logic puzzles are commoditized (NYT bundle, Hearst-owned Puzzmo, an index of 1,462 Wordle-likes); the open edges are social/multiplayer dailies and non-word mechanics. Character/companion apps are bifurcated: Finch (10M users, self-care bird, no neglect penalty) proves a solo-care companion with subscription works, while AR/collect-em-all pets (Peridot dead, TCG Pocket MAU 39M to 23M) are shrinking — nobody has a working shared/group-owned character or a companion tied to real-world friends.

### Cited Findings
- NYT Games app: Wordle, Connections, Strands and the Mini are free; the rest is subscription — [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nyt-games-wordle-crossword/id307569751)
- Puzzmo (Zach Gage, launched Oct 2023, acquired by Hearst Dec 2023) was built to fill the community gap NYT left: leaderboards, friend requests, Discord, daily announcements; free tier + $40/yr — [Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/puzzmo-announcement-zach-gage-interview/); [Game Developer on newspaper games](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-rise-of-newspaper-games)
- The Wordle-like long tail is enormous: one index rates 1,462 daily puzzles — [wordle.today](https://wordle.today/games); a curated 2025 guide of daily puzzle recommendations — [Room Escape Artist, Sep 2025](https://roomescapeartist.com/2025/09/06/daily-puzzle-game-recommendations-guide/)
- Clues By Sam is a recent breakout solo-made daily logic game with a Wikipedia entry — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clues_By_Sam)
- Finch: ~10M users, skews women 25–35; free with Finch Plus $9.99/mo or $69.99/yr; no neglect penalty; 4.9 stars with ~712K App Store ratings and 10M+ Google Play downloads as of June 2026 — [Aidorable review (secondary)](https://www.aidorable.ai/blog/finch-app-reviews); [Internet Matters](https://www.internetmatters.org/advice/apps-and-platforms/wellbeing/finch/)

### Inferences
- Daily-puzzle gaps that remain: (1) two-player/friend-group dailies with a shared result (NYT and Puzzmo are single-player with share-cards; Puzzmo's social layer is leaderboards, not co-play); (2) non-word, non-logic rituals (music, photo, memory, local-knowledge); (3) a daily game whose "result" is a social artifact for a closed group chat (Locket-shaped distribution). All of these are solo-buildable; Wordle and Clues By Sam are solo-made precedents.
- Companion gap: Finch is solitary; Peridot/Pokémon are collect-and-grind. A group-owned creature (a dorm, friend group, or run club shares one pet that grows only when members do real things together or check in) is unoccupied, and fits "belonging" + "charity" (pet grows as the group donates/volunteers). Caveat: Tamagotchi-likes historically have fast decay; Finch avoided it with no-neglect design.
- Rec Room's death and Peridot's sunset mean "social play" has retreated to Roblox/Discord; small, phone-native cooperative play with friends you already have is thin.

### Gaps
- No 2026 Pokémon Go MAU/revenue data found under Scopely.
- No Tamagotchi Uni/app 2026 sales data found.
- Could not find 2026 NYT Games subscriber figures or Puzzmo post-acquisition growth numbers.

## Key Question 5: What are Gen Z and young adults complaining they can't find in 2026 (loneliness, male friendship, dating-app fatigue, no third places)?

### Takeaway
The complaints are well-documented and consistent: 1 in 4 young US men (15–34) report daily loneliness (Gallup), 63%/15% figures on men without close friends circulate, ~79% of Gen Z report dating-app burnout and roughly half have deleted the apps, teens see friends online 60% daily vs 24% in person, and the "third place" framing is now mainstream. The expressed wants are sober, low-cost, recurring, in-person activities with people they already somewhat know — not more matching.

### Cited Findings
- Gallup data (2023–24 aggregate, released 2025): 25% of US men aged 15–34 felt lonely "a lot of the previous day" vs 18% national average and 18% of women the same age; described as the loneliest young male cohort in the Western world — [UVA Today on Gallup](https://news.virginia.edu/content/1-4-young-men-are-lonely-uva-researcher-explains-why-and-how-help); [Fortune, May 21 2025](https://fortune.com/well/2025/05/21/gen-z-millennial-men-loneliness)
- Men: 15% have no close friends vs 10% of women; 1 in 8 adults have no close friends, up from 3% in 1990; time with friends fell 37% 2014–2023 (compiled, secondary) — [ngtimes (secondary)](https://ngtimes.org/2026/04/19/loneliness-study-what-it-tells-men); [friendshiprecession.com (secondary)](https://friendshiprecession.com/friendship-recession-stats-2026/)
- Survey Center on American Life: Gen Z had the loneliest childhoods of any generation — [American Survey Center](https://www.americansurveycenter.org/the-lonely-childhood-of-generation-z/)
- Scape Gen Z Wellbeing Index (Australia): 41% of Gen Z lonely at least weekly; "soft socialising" (cheap, casual) proposed as the fix — [Pedestrian.tv](https://www.pedestrian.tv/culture/scape-gen-z-wellbeing-index-loneliness-epidemic/)
- Dating-app burnout: nearly 4 in 5 report burnout sometimes/often/always; more than half of Gen Z "often or always"; top cause "inability to find a good connection" (40%); 48% of Gen Z deleted all dating apps in 2024; ~156 hrs/yr on apps for ~6 meaningful connections — [ScienceBlog](https://scienceblog.com/t-dating-app-burnout-loneliness-predictors/); [Heresay (secondary)](https://heresay.live/blog/dating-app-burnout-gen-z-2026); 64% of men who used dating apps felt insecure about lack of messages — [Mentor Research Institute](https://www.mentorresearch.org/men-are-disillusioned-with-dating-apps-in-the-us-and-england)
- Pew (Apr 2026): 60% of teens see friends online daily vs 24% in person; ~75% say platforms make them feel more connected to friends; 30% say platforms help friendships vs 7% hurt — [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/04/15/teens-experiences-on-tiktok-instagram-and-snapchat/)
- Gen Z is choosing run clubs, sober activities and Strava over dating apps and Instagram/TikTok — [TechCrunch, Oct 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/12/strava-eyes-ipo-as-gen-z-trades-dating-apps-for-running-clubs/); [SGI Europe](https://www.sgieurope.com/consumer/gen-z-chooses-fitness-over-social-media-strava-study-reveals/118471.article)
- Campus anonymous apps are being banned (UNC system) for bullying — [Gustavian Weekly, Oct 2026](https://weekly.blog.gustavus.edu/2026/10/05/the-toxic-side-of-college-communication-apps/)
- Background: 2023 US Surgeon General advisory labeled loneliness a public-health crisis — cited in [TechCrunch, Apr 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/)

### Inferences
- The demand signal is for turning weak ties into recurring in-person time at near-zero cost, and specifically for men, who "rely on structures that eroded" rather than building intentional ones. An app that supplies the structure (a standing weekly activity with roles, reminders, attendance) is more aligned than another matching app.
- Dating-app fatigue is being absorbed by run clubs, friendship apps and "activity-first" formats; the under-served slice is men 18–30 who will not download a "make friends" app but will join a pickup game, run, or volunteering shift.
- Teens' own view that platforms help friendships (Pew) argues against anti-social-media positioning; position as "more in-person," not "less phone."

## Synthesis: candidate open spaces vs. traps (with solo-founder fit)

### Takeaway
Five candidate gaps score as genuinely open with evidenced demand and no dominant product; six categories look open but are dead or commoditized. Fit for this founder is strongest for group-owned rituals (companion/club/prediction-for-bragging-rights-or-charity) and weakest for anything requiring city-by-city ops or a public feed.

### Cited Findings
(Each candidate below is assembled from citations in Q1–Q6; key anchors repeated inline.)

Candidate open gaps:
1. Private-group, non-money predictions ("call it" with your friends, dorm, club). Demand: $50B real-money volume and Meta's 100M-MAU Arena ambition ([Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/meta-prediction-market-app)); integrity scandals push casual users away from real money ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/22/kalshi-polymarket-comer-insider-trading-probe-congress.html)). Who tried: Manifold (public, forecaster audience, charity-only cash-out) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manifold_(prediction_market))); indie apps Called It/Bernibets/FRR/BeTeam with no evident traction. Who is in it now: Meta (internal, public-feed shaped). Wedge: closed groups + AI-generated markets about the group's own life and local events + leaderboards; optional charity stakes. Why open: no one owns "predictions as group-chat game"; why risky: Arena could ship any day with IG distribution. Fit: high (fun, belonging, charity, no real money), and Expo + LLM resolution is squarely in skill set.
2. Group-owned companion / shared creature. Demand: Finch's 10M users and 4.9-star base ([Aidorable](https://www.aidorable.ai/blog/finch-app-reviews)) prove care-loop retention; Peridot's death ([Peridot](https://playperidot.com/en/news/peridot-mobile-sunset)) and TCG Pocket's slide ([Wargamer](https://www.wargamer.com/pokemon-trading-card-game/pocket-losing-players)) show collect/AR fatigue. Who is in it: Finch (solo), Pokémon Go (Scopely), Tamagotchi (hardware). Wedge: one pet per friend group/club that only grows when members show up IRL, check in, or donate together. Why open: no shipped product found; risk is retention decay typical of pets. Fit: high.
3. Men's recurring activity clubs (non-fitness, sober, free). Demand: 25% daily loneliness among men 15–34 ([UVA/Gallup](https://news.virginia.edu/content/1-4-young-men-are-lonely-uva-researcher-explains-why-and-how-help)), run-club creation +3.5x ([Running Lookout](https://runninglookout.com/news/strava-2026-community-report-running-clubs-3-5x-growth-gen-z/)). Who tried: friendship apps all target women/LGBTQ+/40+/neurodivergent ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/)). Who is in it: Strava clubs (running only), Meetup (old), group chats. Wedge: start-a-micro-club in 60 seconds, standing weekly slot, attendance streaks, no "make friends" branding. Why open: no male-coded, activity-first product; risk is that group chats are "good enough." Fit: high; UCSD is a dense test bed.
4. Multiplayer / friend-group daily ritual game. Demand: NYT and Puzzmo scale; Puzzmo's founder explicitly built for the community gap ([Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/puzzmo-announcement-zach-gage-interview/)). Who is in it: NYT (single-player), Puzzmo (leaderboards), 1,462 Wordle clones ([wordle.today](https://wordle.today/games)). Wedge: a daily that requires 2–6 friends to complete, whose output is a share-card for the group chat; non-word mechanic (photos, music, local trivia, predictions). Why open: co-op dailies are unoccupied; risk is low moat and clone speed. Fit: high; solo-buildable (Wordle, Clues By Sam precedents).
5. Existing-friend-group "what are we doing this week" with commitment mechanics. Demand: Pew 60% online vs 24% in person ([Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/04/15/teens-experiences-on-tiktok-instagram-and-snapchat/)); Eigen's $15M thesis ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/16/exclusive-eigen-seed-round-benchmark-capital-ben-silbermann-mutual-friend/)). Who is in it: Partiful (one-off invites), Yope/Locket (sharing not planning), Eigen (stealth). Wedge: recurring plans, AI that proposes the plan from group chat, streaks/penalties as play money. Why partially open: Eigen is well funded and aimed here; Partiful could add recurrence. Fit: medium-high.

Looks open but is dead/commoditized (avoid):
- Text/link feed "X alternative" (Bluesky −52% actives, Digg dead, X Communities dead) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/blueskys-active-user-base-is-shrinking-as-its-focus-expands-beyond-the-app/)
- AI-generated content social feed (Sora: $1M/day, <500K users) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/29/why-openai-really-shut-down-sora/)
- Stranger dinner/outing matching (Timeleft/222/Pie/Bumble BFF/Meet5; ops-heavy, city-gated, $20–70 per event) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/05/as-people-look-for-ways-to-make-new-friends-here-are-the-apps-promising-to-help/)
- Private photo journal / anti-Instagram (Locket, Retro, Lapse, Yope, BeReal all funded or profitable) — [TechCrunch on Locket](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/06/photo-sharing-app-locket-is-banking-on-a-new-celebrity-focused-feature-to-fuel-its-growth)
- Anonymous campus forum (Fizz/Sidechat duopoly, lawsuits, UNC bans) — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/10/filing-college-app-fizz-accuses-vc-of-sharing-confidential-startup-information-with-rival-sidechat/)
- AR pets / collect-em-all / social VR (Peridot, TCG Pocket decline, Rec Room) — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/niantic-to-shut-down-peridot-mobile-as-ar-ambitions-shift-beyond-smartphones/)
- Real-money prediction markets (regulatory heat, Kalshi/Polymarket scale; also excluded by founder) — [Venable](https://www.venable.com/insights/publications/2026/04/kalshi-and-polymarket-move-to-curb-insider)

### Inferences
- The common denominator of the open gaps is "closed group + recurring ritual + play stakes," which is the Locket/Finch/Strava-club shape, and all three are cheap to run (no video gen, no ops team, no public feed to moderate), matching a solo Expo/AI builder.
- The single biggest external risk across gaps 1 and 5 is Meta (Arena) and Benchmark-backed Eigen respectively; the safest differentiation is local/campus density (UCSD) and charity/IRL mechanics that platform-scale players avoid.
- Nikita Bier's advice argues for narrow-audience launch and a reproducible testing loop; a UCSD-first launch of any of gaps 1–4 fits that.

### Gaps
- No direct evidence that any of the five candidates is currently being built by a funded team beyond Eigen (gap 5) and Meta (gap 1); recommend App Store and Product Hunt sweeps for "predict with friends," "shared pet," and "club streak" before committing.
- Legal review needed for charity-stake mechanics in California.

# Consumer tennis / pickleball / padel app landscape — incumbents (as of October 10, 2026)

Scope: competitor map for a social tennis app for friend groups (character-select roster with joke avatars, courts as "levels" with history, phone-knock match start with photo, ATP-style friend-group season leaderboard, gear log, play-money markets on friends' matches, pickleball overlap, later group kit ordering). US focus, San Diego home market, with European entrants into the US.

Method note: WebSearch/WebFetch only. reddit.com is blocked to this crawler, so Reddit complaints could not be read directly; App Store review quotes (with dates) and Trustpilot/Substack/forum material were used instead. Several corporate pages (GlobeNewswire, Crowdcube, Pickleheads pricing, BusinessWire, Break Sports) returned 403/socket errors; figures from those are reported via secondary sources and flagged.

## Key Question 1 — Playtomic: 2025–2026 US expansion, counts, funding, revenue, what the match/level/social features actually are, US reviews

### Takeaway
Playtomic is the best-funded, most consolidated player (gross bookings ~€240M in 2024, €65M raised March 2025, 6,500+ clubs / 4.7M players / 66+ countries by March–Sept 2026) and is now in the US via padel (102 US clubs, ~26% of US padel clubs, Sept 2026) and, since March 2026, pickleball via DUPR integration. It is a club-booking marketplace with a 0–7 self/peer level and "Open Matches" matchmaking; it has no friend-group seasons, no gear log, no public-court occupancy, and its most recurring complaints are the level algorithm, cancellation/no-show handling, filtering, and club-centric UX.

### Inferences
- Playtomic's "court availability" is bookable-slot inventory for partner clubs only; nothing indicates it shows occupancy of public/municipal courts. In San Diego, where city courts are first-come-first-served (see KQ7), Playtomic's coverage is confined to the handful of private padel/pickleball venues.
- Its social layer is stranger-matchmaking by level, not persistent friend-group competition. A founder's "friend-group season" is orthogonal to Playtomic's marketplace and unlikely to be built there soon (its 2025–26 M&A is all club-SaaS: TIMP, Sportclubby).
- The level-algorithm and no-show complaints are the softest spot; a closed friend-group ladder sidesteps both.

## Key Question 2 — UTR Sports and DUPR: user counts, 2025–2026 funding and business model, consumer app features, complaints

### Takeaway
UTR Sports (Universal Tennis, SF; backers include Larry Ellison, Amazon, Tennis Channel, Djokovic/TEAM8/Endeavor) is a ratings-plus-events company with ~1.7M activated UTRs, a $11.99/mo "Power" subscription, Flex Leagues, and a 2024 pickleball acquisition (PicklePlay). DUPR (Austin; controlled by Agassi/Kass/Raine since Jan 2024, $8M) is the de-facto pickleball rating with "millions" of users, free with a DUPR+ tier and partner/API fees. Both are rating-first, not friend-group-first; complaints centre on rating accuracy/algorithm changes (DUPR, 2025), paid features that "do nothing" (UTR Power), and event ops.

### Inferences
- Both are B2B2C: revenue comes from events, leagues, API/partner licensing and a thin consumer subscription. Neither offers a closed friend-group "season"; UTR Flex Leagues are stranger pools organised by UTR band.
- DUPR's 2025 algorithm backlash (points lost for wins; retroactive adjustments) is a documented pain point that a transparent, friend-group-only Elo could explicitly contrast with.
- The Lucra deal shows "stakes on rec matches" is a live concept in pickleball, but it is real-money and venue-oriented, not play-money among friends.

### Gaps
- A "two million registered users / 183 countries / 350 events in 2026" DUPR figure surfaced in a search summary without an identifiable source URL; treat as unverified.
- No DUPR revenue, 2025–2026 funding, or DUPR+ feature-by-tier breakdown found.
- UTR's true capitalisation and revenue are not public; aggregator numbers conflict.

### Takeaway
The US long tail splits into (a) well-funded pickleball infrastructure (Pickleheads, CourtReserve $54M Oct/Nov 2025, PlayTime Scheduler), (b) one strong AI-video player (SwingVision, Series A 2023 + undisclosed Mar 2026 round), (c) padel booking SaaS tied with Playtomic in the US (Playbypoint), and (d) a graveyard/zombie tier of 2009–2019 tennis social apps (Tennis Round, TennisPAL, Break the Love, TopCourt, GTN) that still exist but show little 2025–26 momentum. ## Key Question 4 — Has any app done "friend group season / ladder" well? (GTN, TennisRungs, Tennis Ladder apps) and health

### Takeaway
No incumbent does a friend-group season well.
